A NOTE ON THE WORK

Connecting knowledge
with meaningful change.

Dr. Asrat Atsedeweyn is an Ethiopian academic leader and associate professor of biostatistics whose work spans university administration, statistical research and public service.

He assumed the presidency of Addis Ababa Science and Technology University in July 2026, following more than 18 years of service at the University of Gondar, including seven years as its president.

His professional interests include strengthening research institutions, expanding access to education, supporting community initiatives and applying quantitative methods to public health questions.
